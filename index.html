Another Axiom Art Community

the Another Axiom Art Community (AAAC) is a community server that aims to provide a safe, welcoming, and postive space for artists who create content for the VR games Gorilla Tag and Orion Drift. We aim to create an enviroment where artists of all kinds- from amature hobbists to professional creators- can share their work and grow their platforms.

Disclaimer

This is a fan-community, and we are no way endorsed or affiliated with Another Axiom or its intellectual properties.

Feel free to hop right into our community! We are always looking for new members who are willing to interact with the community!
